# Code of Conduct

OpenFrame is a student-run project that helps people experiencing homelessness through care packages. We expect everyone (donors, volunteers, coordinators, contributors and maintainers) to:

- Be respectful and assume good faith. No harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Speak about people experiencing homelessness with dignity. They are our neighbours.
- Keep people safe: follow the Safety rules (pairs, daytime, doorstep handoff), and leave any situation that feels unsafe.
- Respect privacy: never share a pickup address, phone number or anything a donor told you. Never record, photograph or describe the people who receive packages.
- Give feedback on code and process, not on people.
- Report problems through a concern report in the app, your chapter coordinator, or the project's configured contact.

Coordinators and maintainers may remove people from a chapter, remove content, or restrict accounts that violate these expectations. This is a plain statement of norms, not a legal document. The maintainers should add a real conduct-contact address here before launch.
