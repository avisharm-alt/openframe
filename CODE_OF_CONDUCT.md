# Code of Conduct

OpenFrame is a student-run project that exists to help people learn. We expect everyone — contributors, reviewers, maintainers and users — to:

- Be respectful and assume good faith. No harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Give feedback on questions and code, not on people. Reviewers explain what to change and why.
- Respect academic integrity: do not share, solicit or discuss actual exam/quiz/test content or answer keys here.
- Respect privacy: do not publish other people's personal information; do not collect student numbers or grades.
- Report problems through the in-app report button, the content-removal page, or the maintainers' configured contact.

Maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, or restrict accounts that violate these expectations. This is a plain statement of norms, not a legal document. The maintainers should add a real conduct-contact address here before launch.
