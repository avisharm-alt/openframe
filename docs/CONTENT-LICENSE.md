**Status: not yet confirmed.** Do not open real submissions until the project owner has chosen and published the terms below.

## Code vs. content

- **Application code** (everything in this repository except accepted question content): proposed **MIT** (see `LICENSE`).
- **Accepted original questions** (stems, options, explanations contributed through the site): proposed **CC BY-SA 4.0**, attributed to "OpenFrame contributors" (or the contributor's chosen pseudonym where they opted in to attribution). Alternatives the owner may prefer: CC BY 4.0 (more permissive) or CC0 (no conditions).
- **Demo seed questions** in `src/lib/seed-data.ts` are demonstration content, licensed with the code under MIT, and never shipped in production.

## Contributor permissions

Every submission requires the contributor to affirm: *"This is original practice content. It is not copied, reconstructed, or adapted from an actual university assessment. I have permission to share the submitted material."* The attestation text and time are stored with each submission.

Before launch the owner should also confirm the wording that tells contributors their accepted question is released under the content license, and that this release is irrevocable for copies already distributed, while the site will stop serving a withdrawn question.

## AI provenance is not ownership

Labelling a question AI-generated or AI-assisted does **not** establish who owns it or whether it can be licensed. Contributors state the facts; the owner decides how to treat AI-generated material under the chosen license. Seek advice from someone qualified. Nothing here is legal advice.
